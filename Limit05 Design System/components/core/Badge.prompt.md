One-line: a pill label for sale, urgency or status — `accent` (Terracotta Rust) is the default and should stay under 5% of the screen.

```jsx
<Badge>Sale -20%</Badge>
<Badge tone="tint">Ripstop</Badge>
```

- Exact geometry: `5px 12px`, `border-radius: 999px`, `font-weight: 700`, `.78rem`.
- Never use accent-on-white body text; the badge is the only place small Terracotta type is acceptable, and only reversed out in white bold.
